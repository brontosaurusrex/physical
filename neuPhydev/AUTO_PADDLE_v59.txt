AUTOMATIC INSPECTION PADDLE — v59
================================

Press O to toggle automatic paddle control. The same option is available in the
E physics/debris tuner.

PURPOSE

This mode lets the game play while you inspect debris, collisions, effects,
level layouts and live tuner changes without manually controlling the paddle.

BEHAVIOR

  * O on a READY screen starts the level.
  * The setting remains enabled across subsequent levels.
  * With two balls, the controller prioritizes the ball predicted to reach the
    paddle line first.
  * It predicts the horizontal landing position and reflects the prediction at
    the side walls.
  * It moves with bounded speed, acceleration and braking; it does not teleport.
  * Prediction is continuously recalculated, so brick impacts and field effects
    are corrected on later physics steps.
  * Manual paddle inputs are ignored while auto paddle is enabled. Press O again
    to return to normal controls.

COMPILE-TIME TUNING

  defaultAutoPaddleMaxSpeed             = 2400
  defaultAutoPaddleAcceleration         = 14000
  defaultAutoPaddleBraking              = 22000
  defaultAutoPaddlePredictionMaxSeconds = 6
  defaultAutoPaddleDeadZone             = 3

These are inspection-controller settings in config.go, not per-level settings.
