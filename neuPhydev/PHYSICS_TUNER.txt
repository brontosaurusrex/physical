IN-GAME PHYSICS TUNER
=====================

Press E to open or close the tuner.

PAUSING

Opening the panel remembers the previous pause state, then pauses the game.
Closing it restores that previous state. Therefore:

  playing -> E -> tuner paused -> E -> playing
  paused  -> E -> tuner paused -> E -> still paused

While the panel is open, normal game keys and canvas pointer controls are
ignored. Range inputs and the export selector still accept keyboard input.

CLIPBOARD EXPORT

Closing with E or the Close and copy button copies one selected format:

  Level-file lines   key=value lines ready above a level's --- separator
  config.go defaults defaultPhysics... assignments for the const block
  Both formats       both blocks with headings

SLIDERS

Motion:
  gravity
  restitution
  airDrag
  magnusCoefficient
  magnusAccelerationScale
  spinDrag

Limits and boosts:
  maxSpeed
  maxSpin
  paddleBoost
  brickBoost

Paddle and spin:
  paddleSpinTransfer
  mousePaddleSpinVelocityLimit
  paddleFrictionScale
  minimumPaddleGrip
  collisionSpinCoupling
  paddleSpinGraceSeconds

Surface contact:
  frictionCoeff
  wallFrictionScale
  brickFrictionScale
  unbreakableFrictionScale
  overspeedHalfLife

Geometry:
  wallTopTiltDegrees
  wallSideTiltDegrees
  brickTiltMaxDegrees

The slider min/max/step values are defined in physicsEditorSliderSpecs in
config.go. They affect only the tuner UI. The normal level parser remains the
authority for accepted level-file values.

RESET BUTTONS

Opening values:
  Restores the complete physicsSettings snapshot from when E opened the panel.

Built-in defaults:
  Restores defaultPhysicsSettings(), including values from config.go.
