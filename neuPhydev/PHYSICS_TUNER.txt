IN-GAME PHYSICS + DEBRIS TUNER — v60
====================================

Press E to open or close the tuner. The panel is docked to the right so most of
the playfield remains visible.

LIVE INSPECTION

Opening the panel preserves the current pause state:

  playing -> E -> game keeps running
  paused  -> E -> game remains paused

The panel has live checkboxes for simulation, auto paddle and debris. O remains
available while the panel is open.

AUTO PADDLE

  autoPaddleHitVariation

This controls how far from the paddle center automatic hits may be planned. A
new stable random offset is selected for each return. 0 always centers; 0.90
uses almost the full paddle width.

DEBRIS GENERATION

  debrisPiecesMin
  debrisPiecesMax
  debrisMaxActivePieces
  debrisLifetime
  debrisLifetimeVariationPercent
  debrisFadeDuration

`debrisLifetimeVariationPercent` applies independently to newly created pieces:

  actual lifetime = base lifetime * (1 +/- percentage/100)

At the default 9 seconds and 16.7%, pieces live approximately 7.5..10.5 seconds.
Changing the percentage affects newly spawned debris. Changing the base lifetime
continues to rescale pieces already in flight while preserving their individual
variation ratio and lifetime progress.

CLIPBOARD EXPORT

Closing with E exports auto-paddle, physics and debris values in the selected
level-file, config.go, or combined format. Opening values and Built-in defaults
also restore the auto hit-variation slider.
