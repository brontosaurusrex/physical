BRICK DEBRIS PHYSICS — v61
==========================

Every destroyed brick produces configurable physical fragments. Each fragment
uses the source brick's main fill color, independent starting opacity, size,
rotation, velocity and lifetime.

LIFETIME VARIATION

Every new piece gets its own lifetime:

  lifetime = debrisLifetime * random(1-v, 1+v)
  v = debrisLifetimeVariationPercent / 100

With 9 seconds and 16.7%, the range is approximately 7.5..10.5 seconds. Use 0
for identical lifetimes. The allowed range is 0..95%.


RENDERING AND COLOR

Debris is drawn behind the cached living-brick layer. Intact bricks therefore
cover fragments, while the empty space left by destroyed bricks reveals them.
Balls, paddle and overlays remain above the debris.

The apparent opacity is pre-blended into an opaque fill color against the
current level background. Pieces retain the muted colors produced by opacity,
but overlapping debris occludes rather than accumulating transparency. The
opening flash and end fade are also represented by opaque blended colors.

SHAPE MIX

New configurable families supplement circles, compact chunks and rare slivers:

  triangles                    3 corners
  stars                        4..7 points / 8..14 corners
  glass-like radial polygons   7..14 corners

Stars vary their inner radius and corner angles. Glass pieces vary both corner
angles and radial lengths, producing sharp, irregular silhouettes. Rendering
uses the full outline; collision remains the existing conservative circle.

COLLISIONS AND FIELDS

Fragments collide with balls, paddle, top/side walls and living bricks after the
configured delay. They never destroy living bricks and do not collide with one
another. Gravity, low/reverse gravity, black holes and magnets affect them.

PERFORMANCE

The active-piece cap remains the main bound. The attached default is 150 pieces.
Mass destruction removes the oldest fragments when necessary.
