BALL BELOW-FLOOR GRACE — V62
============================

The ball remains alive and physically simulated after crossing the visible floor.
It is lost only when its bottom edge travels beyond:

  canvasHeight + ballBelowFloorGracePixels

Default config.go value:

  defaultBallBelowFloorGracePixels = 120.0

Level-file override:

  ballBelowFloorGracePixels=120

Accepted alias:

  ballFloorDeathMargin=120

Range: 0..5000 pixels.

While inside the grace zone, the ball continues receiving:

  - normal or reversed gravity
  - black-hole attraction
  - air drag and Magnus/spin effects
  - side-wall physics if applicable

Therefore a reverse-gravity or black-hole effect can pull the ball back into the
visible playfield before it crosses the final limit.

Set the value to 0 to preserve the old immediate-death boundary.
