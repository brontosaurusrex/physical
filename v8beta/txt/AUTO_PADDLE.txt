AUTOMATIC INSPECTION PADDLE — v60
================================

Press O to toggle automatic paddle control. The same option is available in the
E live tuner.

PURPOSE

This mode lets the game play while you inspect debris, collisions, effects,
level layouts and live tuner changes without manually controlling the paddle.

BEHAVIOR

  * O on a READY screen starts the level.
  * The setting remains enabled across subsequent levels.
  * With two balls, the controller prioritizes the ball predicted to reach the
    paddle line first.
  * It predicts the horizontal landing position and reflects the prediction at
    the side walls.
  * It moves with bounded speed, acceleration and braking; it does not teleport.
  * Manual paddle inputs are ignored while auto paddle is enabled.

VARIABLE HIT POSITION

The controller no longer aims for the center of the paddle every time. It keeps
one randomized target offset stable while approaching a ball, then chooses a new
offset after paddle contact or after switching to the other ball.

  defaultAutoPaddleHitVariation = 0.65

The value is normalized relative to half the paddle width:

  0.00  always aims at the center
  0.65  targets approximately 17.5%..82.5% across the paddle
  0.90  targets approximately 5%..95% across the paddle

The maximum accepted value is 0.90, leaving a safety margin near both edges.
This value is available live in the E panel and may also be set per level:

  autoPaddleHitVariation=0.65

COMPILE-TIME TUNING

  defaultAutoPaddleMaxSpeed             = 2400
  defaultAutoPaddleAcceleration         = 14000
  defaultAutoPaddleBraking              = 22000
  defaultAutoPaddlePredictionMaxSeconds = 6
  defaultAutoPaddleDeadZone             = 3
  defaultAutoPaddleHitVariation         = 0.65
