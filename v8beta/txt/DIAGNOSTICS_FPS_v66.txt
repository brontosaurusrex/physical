DIAGNOSTICS AND FPS DISPLAY — v66
================================

Build ID: 20260803-66d4f9a2c7

P / I DIAGNOSTICS CYCLE

P now cycles through one combined diagnostics sequence:

  first press   physics/performance diagnostics
  second press  level/config/palette diagnostics (the former I view)
  third press   diagnostics off

I remains an alias for the same cycle, preserving the old key while keeping both
views synchronized.

Both full diagnostics views are positioned at the bottom-right. They have no
background rectangle or border, matching the old transparent P presentation.

F MINI FPS DISPLAY

F independently toggles a compact bottom-right display:

  FPS <current smoothed render FPS>
  LOWEST <lowest visible-page sample>

The compact display temporarily hides while either full diagnostics view is
open, preventing overlap. It returns automatically when the full diagnostics
cycle reaches off.

LOWEST FPS MEASUREMENT

The current FPS value remains the existing smoothed render/requestAnimationFrame
rate. LOWEST records the smallest unsmoothed sample measured over a 0.5-second
window.

To avoid meaningless minimums:

  the first two visible-page samples are warm-up only
  hidden/background-tab time is not sampled
  long resume gaps reset the partial sample

LOWEST is session-scoped and resets when the page/game reloads. It measures the
lowest half-second render rate, not the lowest individual-frame instantaneous
rate.

RETAINED OLDER-MACHINE RENDER OPTIMIZATIONS

v66 includes all v65 debris rendering improvements:

  cached Path2D shard outlines
  cached 256-step opaque color palettes
  setTransform-based rendering with less Canvas state churn
  skipped redundant fillStyle assignments
  adaptive drawing density for older, slow shards only
  fresh and high-speed shards always visible
  all debris physics and collisions remain active

The P physics view continues to expose debris draw count, adaptive stride,
learned baseline FPS, and Path2D cache state.
