BREAKOUT v67 — RESETTABLE LOWEST RENDER FPS

Build ID
  20260803-67c1e8b5f4

Controls
  F         Toggle the compact bottom-right FPS display.
  Shift+F   Reset LOWEST FPS without changing whether the display is visible.

Mini display
  FPS       Smoothed current requestAnimationFrame / draw rate.
  LOWEST    Lowest valid visible-page half-second render sample since startup
            or the most recent Shift+F reset.

Reset behavior
  - LOWEST immediately becomes "--".
  - Any partially collected sample is discarded.
  - The normal two-sample warm-up runs again.
  - Hidden-tab time and long resume gaps remain excluded.
  - The compact display stays visible or hidden exactly as it was.

Debris defaults retained for the office-machine test
  defaultDebrisPiecesMin       = 6
  defaultDebrisPiecesMax       = 18
  defaultDebrisMaxActivePieces = 150

Render-cycle improvements retained
  - Cached Path2D shard outlines.
  - Cached 256-step opaque color palettes.
  - setTransform instead of per-shard save/translate/rotate/restore.
  - Redundant fillStyle changes avoided.
  - Adaptive drawing of older slow debris when render FPS falls.
  - Fresh and fast shards always remain visible.
  - Full debris physics and collisions remain active.
  - Slow debris draws behind bricks; fast debris draws in front.

Suggested observation procedure
  1. Press F to show the compact display.
  2. Enter the level or effect you want to measure.
  3. Press Shift+F just before the test.
  4. Trigger debris-heavy gameplay for a repeatable interval.
  5. Record current FPS, LOWEST, debris count, and PHYSICS COMPUTE LOAD.
